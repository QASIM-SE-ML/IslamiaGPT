ISLAMIA COLLEGE PESHAWAR — ADMISSIONS

General Admission Information:
Islamia College Peshawar announces admissions for different academic programs through official admission advertisements and its online admission system.

Applications:
Eligible candidates are required to submit the prescribed application form within the announced admission period. Admission is generally based on merit and the specific eligibility requirements of the program.

Online Application:
Applicants can apply through the official ICP online admission portal:
https://admissions.icp.edu.pk/

Important:
Admission dates, available programs, eligibility requirements, merit criteria, and required documents can vary by program and admission session. Students should check the latest official admission advertisement or guidebook before applying.

BS Admissions:
Islamia College Peshawar offers BS admissions in various departments. The official website publishes the available programs, admission schedules, advertisements, and application links for each session.

FA/FSc Admissions:
Islamia College Peshawar also announces FA/FSc admissions. The university publishes the admission schedule, procedure, guidebook, and online application link on its official website.

Required Documents:
According to the university's admission regulations, applicants may be required to provide documents including:
- SSC/HSSC or equivalent certificates/degrees
- Detailed Marks Certificates (DMCs)
- Character Certificate
- Provisional Certificate
- Domicile Certificate
- Passport-size photographs
- Migration Certificate where applicable
- CNIC, B-Form, or father's/guardian's CNIC
- Required undertaking/declaration
- Other documents specified in the relevant admission advertisement

Important Rule:
The exact documents and eligibility requirements may differ according to the academic program. Applicants should follow the requirements stated in the latest official admission advertisement or prospectus.
