ISLAMIA COLLEGE PESHAWAR — SCHOLARSHIPS

Islamia College Peshawar provides and facilitates different scholarship and financial assistance opportunities for eligible students.

CURRENT / RECENT SCHOLARSHIP OPPORTUNITIES

1. HEC NEED-BASED SCHOLARSHIP
Islamia College Peshawar invites applications from eligible BS students for the Higher Education Commission (HEC) Need-Based Scholarship.

The university publishes application notices and schedules on its official website.

2. ETEA SCHOLARSHIP
Islamia College Peshawar publishes notifications regarding ETEA Scholarship for eligible students, including first-year and second-year students.

3. PAKISTAN BAIT-UL-MAL SCHOLARSHIP
Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal scholarship opportunities have also been announced through Islamia College Peshawar.

4. HBL ENDOWMENT SCHOLARSHIP
The university provides/has provided information and application forms for the HBL Endowment Scholarship.

5. OTHER SCHOLARSHIP OPPORTUNITIES
ICP has previously facilitated scholarships from different governmental and non-governmental organizations, including educational foundations, trusts, alumni organizations, and other financial assistance programs.

IMPORTANT:
Scholarships, eligibility criteria, application dates, available seats, and required documents can change from one academic session to another.

ISLAMIAGPT MUST NOT:
- Guarantee that a student will receive a scholarship.
- Invent scholarship amounts or eligibility criteria.
- Claim that an old scholarship announcement is currently open.
- Give an exact deadline unless it is verified from the latest official announcement.

When a student asks about a current scholarship, IslamiaGPT should use the latest official ICP scholarship announcement or tell the student to check the official ICP website.
