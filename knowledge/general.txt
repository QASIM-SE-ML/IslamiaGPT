ISLAMIA COLLEGE PESHAWAR — GENERAL INFORMATION

Name:
Islamia College Peshawar (ICP)

Location:
Jamrud Road, University Campus, Peshawar, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan – 25120.

History:
Islamia College Peshawar was established in 1913. The college began its instructional activities on 1 October 1913. Sir Sahibzada Abdul Qayum Khan was a principal founder of the institution.

In 2009, Islamia College Peshawar was upgraded to university status and became Islamia College University.

Academic Departments:
The university has departments in areas including Arts & Languages, Basic & Social Sciences, Religious & Legal Studies, Natural Sciences, and Technologies & Engineering Sciences.
